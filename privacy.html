Privacy & control

You decide what's kept.

Sidecar is local-first: it has no server of its own. Your conversation goes only to the AI you already pay for, and everything it remembers lives on your Mac. Here's the honest breakdown.

Your own subscription

Answers come from Claude or Codex through your sign-in — no extra account, no separate bill, no API key.

Nothing to the developer

No server of ours sits in the middle. No analytics, no tracking, no ads. The developer never sees your chats or pages.

On your computer

Conversations and settings are stored on your Mac, and searching them happens there too — your search words are never sent anywhere.

The honest part

What actually leaves your Mac.

Always, no setting: the page text you ask about and your words go to the assistant you chose. That's how it answers.
Your question and its context — with any page text, file, or transcript you included — go from the extension to the companion app over your own computer (127.0.0.1, which never leaves your machine), and from there to the assistant you chose: Claude Code → Anthropic, or Codex → OpenAI, on your own subscription, the same path those tools use themselves.
A web search — when a question needs current information, your assistant runs one, and (only if you turn on "open live web pages") opens pages during it. That goes through Anthropic or OpenAI on your subscription, not any server of ours.
The panel's fonts load from Google Fonts — the same web-font fetch any site using Google Fonts makes, with no conversation content. Turning on the natural read-aloud voice or video listening downloads those components once, from a fixed source checked against a known fingerprint — again, no conversation content. A couple of optional, off-by-default features add a few more cases, listed below. Everything else — your saved chats, searching them, reading aloud, the highlight button, pulling in the page — stays on your computer.

What's kept

Saved on your Mac, controlled by you.

  • Conversations are saved by default so you can reopen them — your messages, text you brought in (a highlight, an attached file's text), the text of pages you chatted about, the titles of those pages, and a web answer's source links. You can turn saving off in Settings.
  • Deleting a conversation and "forget this page" (which removes a page's saved text and remembered title) live on the companion app's admin page — open it from Settings → "Open admin page ↗".
  • Pictures and audio transcripts are kept only if you switch on their off-by-default settings.
  • A log file is written only if you set the app to start automatically at login; it lives in the same permission-protected folder (readable only by you) and isn't written when you launch the app yourself.

Never

What Sidecar does not do.

  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • It does not use your data for advertising, profiling, or credit decisions.
  • It does not send your data to the developer, or to anyone other than the assistant you chose — Anthropic for Claude, OpenAI for Codex — via your own subscription.
  • It loads no remote code; all of the extension's code ships inside the extension package.
